About

A voice
in the cello

Kesara Bunayamongkol is a Thai cellist whose musical journey began at the age of seven. A scholarship recipient at the San Francisco Conservatory of Music, she is now pursuing her Master of Music at the Royal College of Music in London under the tutelage of Alexander Boyarsky.

As a founding member of the Asterios Piano Trio, she has performed at some of the world's most celebrated stages — bringing together precision, passion, and a deep commitment to classical chamber music.
